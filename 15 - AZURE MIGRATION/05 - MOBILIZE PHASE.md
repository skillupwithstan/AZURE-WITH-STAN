MIGRATION SERIES - THE PHASES OF MIGRATION (ENGLISH)

Before migrating any workloads to the cloud, organizations must adopt a structured and phased approach to guarantee success. This video breaks down the fundamental phases of migration and the critical components needed to execute them smoothly.

Key Learning Outcomes:

The 6 Generic Phases of Migration: Discover, Assess, Plan, Migrate, Optimize, and Operate/Secure.

Microsoft Azure’s 5 Migration Phases: Migration Preparation & Business Planning, Portfolio Discovery & Planning, Migration & Validation, Operate/Optimize/Scale, and Handover/Release.

The PPT Framework: Why migration isn't just about tools. Success relies heavily on People (Cloud Center of Excellence, stakeholders, skilled partners), Process (change management, knowledge base, backup strategy, risk mitigation), Technology (selecting the right replication tools, networking, and identity integration), and Methodology (building a business case, executive sponsorship, Pilot/POCs).

Importance of Discovery: Why extracting inventory and configuration management (CMDB) data (using tools like ServiceNow) forms the backbone of your entire migration strategy.
