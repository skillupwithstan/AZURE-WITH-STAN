MIGRATION SERIES IN ENGLISH - MIGRATION BEST PRACTICES
This video outlines the crucial best practices and pre-migration checks a migration engineer must follow to ensure a smooth, error-free cloud migration process.

Key Learning Outcomes:

Special Handling & Runbooks: Utilizing special handling runbooks for validation, verification, and pre-migration surveys.

Source Cleanup & Optimization: Deleting unnecessary temporary files, uninstalling unwanted tools/drivers, and stopping conflicting services or scheduled tasks to prevent replication slowdowns.

Security & Antivirus Settings: Temporarily disabling antivirus or anti-malware tools and BitLocker encryption during data replication to avoid blocking data flow.

Network & Port Configurations: Verifying network throughput, latency, Jumbo Frames, MTU settings, and ensuring required ports (like 443 and 115) are open at both the network firewall and OS firewall levels.

Licensing & ITSM Audits: Validating OS and application licensing, checking ITSM/ServiceNow for any open problem tickets or prior incidents on source servers, and strictly adhering to change management windows and approvals.
