AZURE MIGRATE IN TAMIL - DISCOVER PHASE
This comprehensive tutorial dives into the foundational stage of cloud migration: the Discover and Assess Phase using Azure Migrate. Perfect for cloud engineers and architects, this session covers:

Migration Methodologies: Understanding the people, processes, and products needed for a successful migration (including Change Management and Business Cases).

Discovery Tools & Options: Exploring agentless vs. agent-based discovery, utilizing the Azure Migrate Appliance for Hyper-V/VMware, and importing data offline via RVTools or CSV templates.

Building a Business Case: Learn how to translate discovered inventory into a tangible Total Cost of Ownership (TCO) and ROI report for stakeholders.

Migration Readiness: Step-by-step practical demonstration on generating assessment reports, grouping servers, and setting up the Hyper-V host for replication.

Whether you're handling a small data center exit or a complex enterprise consolidation, this video outlines exactly how to gather the right data and assess your environment before hitting "migrate."
