This session explains how students can get hands-on experience with top cloud platforms (AWS, Microsoft Azure, and Google Cloud) completely for free, without needing a credit or debit card. It breaks down how to leverage programs like AWS Educate, Azure for Students, and Google Cloud Education Grants to claim free credits and access official training labs.

Key takeaways include:

How to sign up using a college/school email ID or a standard email account.

Accessing core cloud building blocks (Compute, Storage, Network, IAM, and Security) at zero cost.

Setting up billing alerts and using Cost Explorer to manage free credits safely without burning through them.

Earning shareable completion badges to highlight your hands-on cloud skills on LinkedIn.
